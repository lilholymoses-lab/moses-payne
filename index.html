Welcome to The Geometry of Becoming

The Geometry of Becoming is an ongoing collection of short essays exploring the structure and purpose of reality through mathematical structures, scientific concepts, philosophical reasoning, and theological perspectives.

These essays are being developed as part of a larger book that will eventually bring the different pieces together into one continuous work. For now, I am sharing them individually as the ideas develop.

The project is organized into three main movements.

Project 1: The Cosmic Purpose begins from within. It explores reality as a process of becoming, beginning with unity and moving through differentiation, possibility, relationship, becoming, and new potential. The central question is whether relationship is not simply something that exists within reality, but something fundamental to how reality itself becomes.

The Bridge will then explore how these ideas can be expressed through mathematical and geometric language. It will serve as a connection between the metaphysical ideas developed in Project 1 and the scientific structures examined in Project 2.

Project 2 will turn outward toward science. It will explore how organized physical structures emerge across different levels of reality, asking whether the patterns of relationship described philosophically can also be recognized in the structures studied by science.

This project has taken a great deal of time, thought, and personal reflection to develop. I am still working through many of these ideas, and I expect them to continue developing as I learn more and encounter new questions.

If you have found your way here and are taking the time to read, thank you. Whether you agree with the ideas, question them, or simply find something interesting in them, your willingness to engage with the work means a great deal to me.

I hope you’ll explore the project and, most importantly, think alongside it.

— Moses
