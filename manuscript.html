The Manuscript

A work in progress — a Gnostic-style spiritual scripture on the beginning of reality, to which I will continuously be adding.

In the Beginning of Reality

1 Before the heavens and the earth came into being, the Undivided Potential was.

2 It was not emptiness, as one might suppose, for within it lay the possibility of all that might become.

3 Yet no form had appeared, nor had there arisen any boundary by which one thing might be distinguished from another.

4 For all remained within the fullness of the Undivided, and nothing that might become had yet appeared in its own form.

5 Let us therefore speak of how distinction was revealed, and how the many might emerge without the Whole being divided.

6 For the Undivided Potential was whole, yet its fullness was not exhausted by its wholeness.

7 Within it lay the possibility of distinction, and within distinction lay the possibility of expression.

8 And the Undivided Potential differentiated, not by dividing itself, but by revealing what had always been within it.

9 Thus Unity was revealed, the wholeness in which all distinction remained contained.

10 And Possibility was revealed, the openness through which that wholeness might express what had not yet appeared.

11 These were distinguishable, yet neither stood apart from the Source, nor had the Source ceased to be whole.

12 For distinction had arisen, but separation had not; what was revealed remained within that from which it was revealed.

13 And through this distinction, the eternal nature of the Undivided became apparent.

14 This nature was Relationship, which had never been absent from the Source, though its generative character had not yet been revealed.

15 For Relationship was not another being brought forth beside the Undivided, but the eternal nature through which distinction might become generative.

16 Unity was that which remained whole, and Possibility was that through which the unexpressed might emerge.

17 And through their Relationship, what had remained unexpressed could begin to take form.

18 But Relationship did not end with the first distinction, but opened the way for further distinctions to arise.

19 That which emerged became the condition for what might emerge thereafter, and each expression opened the way for another.

20 Thus Relationship was generative, and its movement gave rise to an order through which forms might come to be.

21 And through this order, structure became possible, and the conditions of physical existence began to unfold.

22 For Possibility was not yet actuality, nor was that which might become the same as that which had taken form.

23 Yet through Relationship, the possible could enter into conditions by which it might become actual.

24 And the physical universe emerged, not from an absolute nothing, but as an expression of the eternal movement of becoming.

25 And the heavens spread forth, and worlds arose within the order through which physical forms could exist.

26 And from simpler forms arose greater complexity, until conditions appeared through which life might emerge.

27 And life sustained itself through relationships, preserving its form through the very conditions upon which its existence depended.

28 And within life, relationship became inwardly expressed, until the living could perceive something of its own existence.

29 Thus that which had unfolded throughout the heavens became manifest within the living, and the universe came to behold itself through the forms that had arisen within it.

30 And so the smallest and the greatest bore the mark of the same unfolding, each expressing in its own manner the movement through which forms arise.

31 And though their forms were many, the movement through which they arose was not divided into separate beginnings.

32 Understand, then, that the universe did not bring becoming into existence, nor did physical form give birth to the eternal nature of Relationship.

33 For the universe is not the beginning of Relationship, but its eternal becoming made manifest.
